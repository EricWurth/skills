# Form Answers

Standing answers for application forms. Two tiers — the distinction matters:

- **Tier 1 (standing facts):** stable, factual, safe to reuse anywhere.
- **Tier 2 (judgment answers):** comp numbers, essay answers, legal attestations,
  consents — ALWAYS drafted for your approval per application, never auto-filled or
  auto-submitted. You submit every application yourself.

## Tier 1 — standing facts (fill in)

- Legal name:
- Email:
- Phone:
- City / State / ZIP:
- LinkedIn URL:
- Work authorization (e.g., "US citizen", "authorized, no sponsorship needed"):
- Require visa sponsorship now or in future: yes/no
- Willing to relocate: (mirror your match-profile location gate)
- Notice period / earliest start:
- How did you hear about us: "Company careers site" (or the actual board)
- Veteran status / disability / demographic questions: (your standing choices,
  including "prefer not to answer" where that's your preference)

## Tier 2 — judgment strategy (fill in the strategy, not canned answers)

- **Salary expectation strategy:** e.g., "Deflect with 'flexible depending on the
  total benefits package'; if a number is forced, ask for `<target>`, floor
  `<walk-away>` (from match-profile). Never state the floor in a form."
- **"Why this company" essays:** drafted per application from the posting and your
  master resume; 3–5 sentences, concrete, no flattery boilerplate.
- **References:** who may be listed, and who must be asked first.
